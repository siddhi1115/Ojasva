Why OJASVA?

OJASVA is an AI-powered Energy Intelligence Platform designed to help users understand electricity usage without installing individual sensors on every appliance. Using Non-Intrusive Load Monitoring (NILM), Deep Learning, and intelligent analytics, the platform predicts appliance-wise energy consumption, estimates electricity bills, detects unusual behaviour, and recommends energy-saving actions.
